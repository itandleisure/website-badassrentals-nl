E-chopper rental in Giethoorn

Fat tyres, a low seat and not a sound. Rent an e-chopper from € 32.50 and cruise through Weerribben-Wieden like a real badass.

Check availability & prices

The electric Harley

An e-chopper is an electric scooter in chopper style: wide tyres, a comfortable seat and handlebars you sit relaxed behind. It rides at about 25 km/h and is fully electric, so all you hear is nature around you.

From our meeting point at Restaurant Hollands Venetië you're in Weerribben-Wieden National Park within minutes. You ride past picture-perfect spots like Belt-Schutsloot, Wanneperveen, Kalenberg and Blokzijl, with wide views over the water along the way. It's the quiet side of Giethoorn that most day visitors never see.

  • More than 30 e-choppers, perfect for groups
  • Phone holder and USB charger on every e-chopper
  • Checked and serviced regularly
  • Breakdown? We come straight away with a replacement
  • And of course that badass photo of you on the chopper

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Book online

    Choose the date, start time and how long you want to ride in our booking system. Want to ride today? Give us a call or send an email.

  2. Step 2

    Come to the start

    Meet us at Hollands Venetië, Beulakerweg 167 in Giethoorn. Free parking on site. Bring your driving licence.

  3. Step 3

    Ride!

    A short explanation, phone in the holder, start your route and off you go. As long as you're back on time, you can ride wherever you like.

Routes

You're free to go wherever you want, but we've also mapped out routes past the most beautiful spots of Weerribben-Wieden: along the water, through the woods and over quiet dykes.

  1. Download the Routefabriek app (Route.nl) from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Search for badass.
  3. Choose a route and tap route volgen (follow route).

Inspiration for your ride: cruise past the villages of Dwarsgracht and Belt-Schutsloot, or through the Weerribben to peaceful Kalenberg. More ideas in things to do in Giethoorn.

Rental conditions

Driving licence required. You need a valid car driving licence. A car licence from your home country is fine. Please bring it with you.

  • Wearing a helmet is mandatory on the e-chopper.
  • A regular e-chopper is for one person. Taking someone along? Choose a duo e-chopper.
  • Stay on paved roads and cycle paths.
  • The centre of Steenwijk is off limits (camera enforcement, € 210 fine).
  • The e-chopper has third-party insurance; the renter's excess is € 1,000. Rather not worry? Add the damage and theft cover (from € 3.50 per vehicle) when you book.

See also our FAQ. No driving licence? Rent a fat bike instead.

Tip: on sunny days bring sunglasses and sunscreen, on cooler days a jacket and gloves, and a poncho if it might rain.
