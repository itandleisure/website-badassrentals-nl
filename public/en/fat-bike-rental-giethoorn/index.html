What is an electric fat bike?

A fat bike is an electric bicycle with extra-wide tyres. The tyres roll smoothly over any cycle path and give you lots of comfort, while the motor gives you a solid push with every pedal stroke. The result: easy kilometres, even against the wind on the dyke.

The best part? You don't need a driving licence. That makes the fat bike the ideal way to rent a bike in Giethoorn, and perfect for groups where not everyone has a licence. Mix and match: part of the group on e-choppers, the rest on fat bikes.

  • No driving licence needed
  • Minimum height 1.50 m
  • 15 fat bikes available, choose your own start time
  • Helmet not required, but recommended

Giethoorn and beyond by bike

Giethoorn, the 'Venice of the North', is famous for its canals, wooden bridges and thatched farmhouses. But the real adventure starts just outside the village: Weerribben-Wieden National Park is the largest peat wetland in north-west Europe. Think endless reed beds, open water and a rich bird life.

After your ride, grab a seat on the terrace of Restaurant Hollands Venetië, our meeting point. It's open every day, so you can stop for food and drinks before or after your ride.

Tip: cycle on to peaceful Kalenberg, which shows how Giethoorn looked in the old days. More ideas in things to do in Giethoorn.

Fat bike questions

Do I need a driving licence for a fat bike?

No. An electric fat bike is an e-bike with pedal assist up to 25 km/h, so no licence is needed. You do need to be at least 1.50 m tall.

Can children ride a fat bike?

Yes, there is no minimum age. Anyone 1.50 m or taller can ride. Smaller children can ride along on the back of a duo e-chopper.

Do I have to wear a helmet?

A helmet is not required on the fat bike, but it's a good idea.

How far can I go on a fat bike?

The Ouxi V8 battery has more than enough range for a full day of riding through Giethoorn and Weerribben-Wieden. Ask us for the best route when you pick up your bike.

Rental conditions

  • You are at least 1.50 m tall. There is no minimum age, so teenagers are welcome.
  • A fat bike is for one person; no passengers or children on the back.
  • Maximum speed is 25 km/h; please follow the Dutch traffic rules.
  • The centre of Steenwijk is off limits (camera enforcement, € 210 fine).
  • The fat bike is insured; the renter's excess is € 1,000. Rather not worry? Add the damage and theft cover (from € 3.50 per vehicle).
