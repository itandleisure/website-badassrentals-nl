From Giethoorn straight into nature

Our e-choppers and fat bikes are ready for you at Restaurant Hollands Venetië on the Beulakerweg in Giethoorn. From there you ride straight into Weerribben-Wieden National Park: past reed beds, lakes and little bridges, to villages like Belt-Schutsloot, Wanneperveen and Blokzijl.

Follow one of our GPS routes on your phone, or simply ride wherever you like. As long as you're back on time, it's all good. Afterwards, the terrace is waiting for a bite and a drink.

Good to know

{% faq %} Q: Do I need a driving licence? A:

For the e-chopper, yes: a valid licence for a car, motorcycle or moped. Please bring it with you. For the fat bike you don't need a licence, but you must be at least 1.50 m tall.

Q: Is a foreign driving licence OK? A:

Yes, a valid driving licence from your home country for a car, motorcycle or moped is fine.

Q: How fast do the e-choppers go? A:

About 25 km/h. You ride on the cycle paths and can go anywhere a regular bicycle can.

Q: Can I take a child or passenger with me? A:

Yes, on our special duo e-chopper two people ride together. A regular e-chopper and a fat bike are for one person only.
