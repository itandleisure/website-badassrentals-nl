Things to do in Giethoorn: tips from locals

Giethoorn, the 'Venice of the North', attracts visitors from all over the world. But what do you actually do there? We live and work here, so here are our tips: from the famous classics to places most tourists never see.

1. Take a boat through the canals

The classic. The old village of Giethoorn has no roads, only canals with wooden bridges and thatched farmhouses. Join a canal tour or rent a whisper boat or open boat from one of the boat rental companies in the village.

2. Walk along the canals

A footpath with countless wooden bridges runs along the canals. Perfect for a relaxed stroll past the beautiful houses and gardens. Tip: come early in the day or on a weekday, when it's much quieter.

3. Ride an e-chopper through Weerribben-Wieden

The best of Giethoorn starts just outside the village. On an e-chopper, an electric scooter in chopper style, you cruise quietly through Weerribben-Wieden National Park: past reed beds, open water and meadows. You do need a driving licence.

4. Rent a bike: the electric fat bike

No driving licence? Hop on an electric fat bike. You pedal yourself, but with electric assist, so it's effortless. The Netherlands is made for cycling, and this is the most fun way to rent a bike in Giethoorn.

5. Boat and ride in one day

Can't choose? On our Discover Giethoorn & Weerribben tour you ride the e-chopper and cruise in a luxury open boat, with coffee, apple pie and lunch included. In the evening there's the Evening Chopper Tour.

6. Discover the quiet neighbouring villages

Dwarsgracht and Belt-Schutsloot are close by and just as pretty as Giethoorn, but far less busy. Kalenberg, in the middle of the Weerribben, shows how Giethoorn looked in the old days. On an e-chopper or fat bike you're there in no time.

7. Eat on a terrace by the water

Giethoorn has lots of restaurants and terraces by the water. Our meeting point, Restaurant Hollands Venetië on the Beulakerweg, is open every day: ideal for lunch or a drink before or after your ride.

Practical tips

  • Getting there: Giethoorn is about 1.5 hours by car from Amsterdam. By public transport, take the train to Steenwijk and then the bus to Giethoorn.
  • Parking: there is plenty of parking at our meeting point at Hollands Venetië.
  • Crowds: the village is busy in summer and at weekends. Head out into Weerribben-Wieden to escape the crowds.
  • Weather: bring sunglasses and sunscreen on sunny days, and a rain jacket if in doubt.
