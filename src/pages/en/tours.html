Giethoorn tours: e-chopper & boat

The best of both worlds: on the water in a luxury open boat and on land on the e-chopper. We arrange everything, you enjoy.

{% img groepsuitje-e-choppers-blokzijl "Group of smiling people on e-choppers in the centre of Blokzijl" sizes="(max-width: 860px) 100vw, 45vw" %}
Full day

Discover Giethoorn & Weerribben

  • 10:00 - 16:30
  • From 2 people
  • From € 69.50 p.p.

The complete Giethoorn day out, with everything taken care of. You start with coffee and Dutch apple pie, then head out on the e-chopper for 2.5 hours, past idyllic villages like Belt-Schutsloot and Blokzijl. After lunch you step into a luxury open boat for a 2.5-hour trip through Giethoorn.

  1. 10:00Welcome with coffee and apple pie
  2. 10:302.5-hour e-chopper ride through Weerribben-Wieden
  3. 13:00Lunch: choose a sandwich from the lunch menu
  4. 14:002.5-hour boat trip through Giethoorn and Weerribben-Wieden
  5. 16:30End of the tour

Evening Chopper Tour

  • 17:45 - 21:30
  • From 2 people
  • From € 57.00 p.p.

  2. 18:001.5 hours riding the e-chopper
  3. 19:30A drink on the terrace
